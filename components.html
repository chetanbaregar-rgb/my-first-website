Design Guidelines

Build with clarity and purpose

A comprehensive guide to designing experiences for India's leading B2B circular economy marketplace — where material transparency meets business efficiency.

Design Philosophy

recykal.market connects buyers and sellers of secondary materials through a platform built on transparency, trust, and efficiency. Every design decision serves these core values.

◐
Clarity First
Material specifications, pricing, and availability must be instantly clear. Ambiguity erodes trust in B2B transactions.
✓
Earned Trust
Verification badges, transaction history, and quality certifications build confidence. Trust is shown, not claimed.
⚡
Efficient Workflows
Time is currency. Streamlined listing creation, intelligent search, and rapid transaction flows respect our users' expertise.
↻
Circular Impact
Make environmental impact visible without greenwashing. Real metrics, honest progress, meaningful contribution to circularity.

Design Principles

1. Enterprise Sophistication

Our users are business professionals making high-value decisions. The interface reflects the gravity of these transactions through refined typography, purposeful use of white space, and a sophisticated color palette that signals credibility.

2. Data-Dense Clarity

Unlike consumer marketplaces, B2B users need comprehensive information upfront. Material specifications, quality certifications, pricing history, and seller credentials should be scannable yet detailed. Progressive disclosure reveals complexity without overwhelming initial impressions.

3. Honest Sustainability

Circular economy metrics matter, but only when they're verifiable. Show carbon offsets, material circularity rates, and landfill diversion — but always with data sources. Impact visibility without empty claims.

4. Refined Efficiency

Speed doesn't require sacrifice of beauty. Optimized loading, intelligent caching, and streamlined interactions create efficiency that feels effortless, not rushed.

Remember
Every pixel should either inform, guide, or build trust. Decoration for its own sake has no place in a tool designed for business.

Typographic Hierarchy

  • Material names: Title 1 (40px Bold) for primary focus, Title 2 (28px Semibold) for category headers
  • Body copy: 17px Regular with generous line height (1.65) for readability in long descriptions
  • Metadata: 13-15px Regular/Medium in Gray 600 for timestamps, locations, quantities
  • Data values: SF Mono for prices, weights, and SKU codes — monospace aids comparison
  • Buttons: 15px Medium, letter-spacing -1% for optimal legibility
Type Pairing
Use Inter for all interface text. Reserve SF Mono exclusively for technical values (prices, quantities, codes, timestamps). Never mix multiple display fonts — consistency signals professionalism.

Motion

Motion should feel responsive and purposeful, never gratuitous. Use animation to guide attention, provide feedback, and create spatial relationships.

Timing & Easing

  • Swift (0.2s): Hover states, button presses, toggle switches — immediate feedback
  • Standard (0.3s): Card reveals, dropdown menus, modal appearance — comfortable pacing
  • Deliberate (0.4s): Page transitions, data loading, complex animations — unhurried elegance
  • Easing: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) for all transitions (Apple's standard easing)

Motion Principles

  • No animation by default: Motion is opt-in. Static by default respects user preferences and performance.
  • Consistent easing: Use one easing curve throughout. Visual consistency matters more than varied effects.
  • Respect reduced motion: Always check prefers-reduced-motion and provide instant fallbacks.
  • Performance first: Transform and opacity only. Avoid animating layout properties (width, height, top, left).

Imagery

Photography should be authentic, unstyled, and focused on materials and processes. Avoid stock imagery — real materials, real facilities, real people.

Photography Style

  • Materials: Clean, well-lit product shots. Show texture and quality. Natural lighting preferred.
  • Facilities: Wide shots showing scale and cleanliness. Highlight sorting, processing, and quality control.
  • People: Candid over posed. Show actual work — inspecting materials, operating equipment, reviewing documentation.
  • Environmental context: Avoid greenwashing. If showing environmental benefit, use data visualization, not forests and leaves.

Image Guidelines

✓ Do

Authentic material photography

  • Clear, detailed shots of actual materials
  • Consistent lighting and backgrounds
  • Multiple angles showing quality
  • Real facility photos
✗ Don't

Generic stock imagery

  • Stock photos of people pointing at screens
  • Generic green nature backgrounds
  • Overly processed or filtered images
  • Symbolic representations instead of real materials

Forms

Forms should feel lightweight and intelligent. Use smart defaults, contextual help, and progressive validation to reduce cognitive load.

Input Fields

Be specific about material type and quality

Form Guidelines

  • Label positioning: Above input, 8px margin. Never inside inputs as placeholders.
  • Required fields: Asterisk (*) next to label, explain at form top
  • Help text: Below input, Gray 600, 13px. Contextual, not repetitive.
  • Validation: Inline on blur, not on every keystroke. Show success subtly.
  • Error states: Red border (1.5px), error icon, clear message explaining how to fix
  • Focus states: Green border (2px), slight shadow, smooth transition

Table Guidelines

  • Sorting: All columns sortable unless contextually inappropriate (actions)
  • Alignment: Left for text, right for numbers, center for icons/actions
  • Zebra striping: Avoid. Use subtle hover states instead.
  • Row height: Comfortable padding (16px vertical minimum)
  • Monospace numbers: Essential for price/quantity columns — aids comparison
  • Sticky headers: On long tables, header stays visible during scroll

Voice & Tone

Our voice is professional yet approachable, data-focused but human, and always rooted in circular economy expertise.

Voice Characteristics

Clear & Direct
No jargon without definition. "Verified quality standards" not "holistic sustainability alignment."
Expert but Accessible
We know circular economy deeply. We explain it clearly. Depth without pretension.
Action-Oriented
"List your materials" not "Begin your sustainability journey." Verbs over vague aspirations.
Honest & Grounded
We show data, not claims. Impact metrics over marketing language. Trust through transparency.

We Are

  • Confident but not arrogant — We're India's leading platform. Our data quality shows it; we don't need to say it.
  • Precise with terminology — "Post-consumer PET" not "recycled plastic." Specificity builds trust.
  • Helpful without being condescending — Explain when needed, assume expertise when appropriate.
  • Focused on outcomes — What users accomplish matters more than our platform features.

We Are Not

  • ❌ Greenwashing or making vague sustainability claims
  • ❌ Overly casual or consumer-tech playful
  • ❌ Corporate jargon or unnecessarily complex
  • ❌ Feature-focused without business value
  • ❌ Making promises we can't verify with data
